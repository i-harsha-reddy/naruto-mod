# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Twenty ninja from the series, each drawn by hand in pixels, with three
  jutsu, a quick strike and a cheer of their own, on a 48×12 stage above the
  Claude Code prompt.
- The ninja reacts to Claude's work: a quick strike for an edit or a short
  command, the signature jutsu for a command that runs a while, a summoning
  or clones for a subagent, a cheer when a turn ends, a slump when something
  fails, and sleep when you step away.
- The spinner speaks in the ninja's voice and shouts each jutsu as it lands.
- `/ninja` to pick a ninja, watch a jutsu, or turn it down (`quiet`) or off.
- Smaller terminals get a close-up of the ninja that follows the action; the
  desktop app draws the same pixel art.
